---
title: How spicy is the paste? Can I adjust it?
category: Product
keywords: spicy, spice level, hot, mild, pedas, heat, children, kids
---

Each paste is a **flavour base rather than a chilli paste**. The focus is aroma and depth, so the heat is moderate and most families find it comfortable.

To adjust:

- Use less paste for a milder, more fragrant dish
- Add more for extra depth and heat
- Balance a dish that came out too strong with more rice, yoghurt or coconut milk

Kabsah is the gentlest of the three. Briyani carries the boldest spice.
