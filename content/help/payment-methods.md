Checkout is handled by **CHIP**, a Malaysian payment gateway. Depending on what CHIP offers for your account you can pay by:

- FPX online banking
- Credit and debit card
- Supported e-wallets

Your card and banking details are entered on CHIP's secure payment page and are never stored by us.
