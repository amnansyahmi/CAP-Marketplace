---
title: Can I return or exchange an order?
category: Orders
keywords: refund, return, exchange, cancel, damaged, wrong item, money back, broken jar
---

Because these are food products, **opened jars cannot be returned**.

If something arrives damaged, or you receive the wrong item, contact us within **48 hours of delivery** with your order reference and a photo, and we will put it right with a replacement or a refund.

To change or cancel an order, get in touch as quickly as possible — once a parcel has been dispatched we can no longer amend it.
