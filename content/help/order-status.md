---
title: How do I check my order?
category: Orders
keywords: order status, track, tracking, reference number, confirmation, where is my order, receipt
---

When your order is placed we show a **reference number** that looks like `CA-7F3K9Q`, and send a confirmation to the email address you entered.

Keep that reference — it identifies your order if you need to get in touch.

If a payment did not complete, nothing is charged and the order is simply left unpaid. You can place it again whenever you are ready.
