Orders to {{delivery.westLabel}} usually arrive within **2 to 4 working days**.

Deliveries to {{delivery.eastLabel}} typically take a little longer, around **4 to 7 working days**.

Orders are packed on working days. Anything placed over a weekend or public holiday is dispatched the next working day.
