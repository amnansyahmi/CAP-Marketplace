We make three: {{product.names}}. Each is {{price.from}} for a {{product.weight}} jar.

- **Kabsah Paste** — tomato-forward, aromatic and warmly spiced. The gentlest of the three and the easiest place to start.
- **Mandy Paste** — smoky and fragrant with a lighter spice, made for fluffy basmati and roast chicken.
- **Briyani Paste** — the boldest, with toasted spice and deep savoury aroma. Good for celebration rice.

If you are buying your first jar, most people begin with Kabsah.
