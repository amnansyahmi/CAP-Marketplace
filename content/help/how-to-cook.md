---
title: How do I cook with the paste?
category: Cooking
keywords: how to use, recipe, cooking instructions, method, prepare, steps, masak
---

Every paste works the same way, in three steps:

- **Sauté** the paste in a little oil until it turns fragrant
- **Build** the dish by adding your protein, rice and water
- **Gather** everyone once the rice is fluffed and ready to serve

A {{product.weight}} jar seasons roughly 1kg of rice or protein.

Start with less than you think you need — you can always stir in more, but you cannot take it out.
