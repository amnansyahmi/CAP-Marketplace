---
title: How should I store the paste?
category: Product
keywords: storage, keep, refrigerate, fridge, shelf life, expire, how long does it last, spoil
---

Keep unopened jars in a **cool, dry place** away from direct sunlight. Stored that way they keep well until the date printed on the label.

Once opened, **refrigerate the jar** and use it within the period stated on the label.

- Always use a clean, dry spoon
- Close the lid firmly after each use
- Do not leave the jar out at room temperature for long periods

If the paste smells or looks different from usual, do not use it.
